Clarilux Candidate Guide
Welcome to Clarilux, an advanced oral board simulation platform designed to evaluate and elevate clinical competence. Clarilux provides a realistic, high-fidelity environment that mirrors professional medical board examinations, allowing candidates to demonstrate their clinical reasoning, decision-making, and procedural knowledge through structured digital simulations.
Whether you are navigating a complex patient case or completing a timed evaluation, this application serves as your secure, intuitive testing gateway. This comprehensive guide will walk you through every phase of the platform workflow, from initial registration to the final submission of your simulation performance.

Oral Board
The Oral Board is a high-fidelity simulation environment designed to bridge the gap between textbook knowledge and the high-pressure environment of board examinations. By simulating the structure of a real examination, the Oral Board helps you refine your clinical reasoning, verbalize complex decision-making processes, and manage the rapid-fire nature of board-style questioning.
Key Features and TopicsThe platform covers a comprehensive range of clinical domains to ensure you are prepared for any scenario. Some topics currently available include:
	•	Cardiovascular & Neuro: High-stakes management and physiology.
	•	Obstetrics: Specialized care and maternal-fetal considerations.
	•	Pain Management & Pharmacology: Deep dives into drug interactions and therapeutic pathways.

How To Start Your First Session Navigating to your simulation is straightforward. Follow these steps to begin:
	•	Log In: Access your account via the secure login page.
	•	Access Oral Board Sessions On the left-side menu (which includes Dashboard, Oral Board, Question Bank, Marketplace, and Analytics), click on Oral Board.
	•	Choose a subject from the available options. E.g, Endocrine.

Review Session Summary
	•	The screen will display a summary of the session, indicating it includes 2 cases, a 10-minute case study review period before each case, and 35 minutes of case time (comprising 30 minutes of focused questioning and a 5-minute grab-bag session at the end).
	•	Click Begin Session.
Configure Session Experience
	•	While the first case is preparing, choose your preferred interaction method: Avatar, Voice, or Text.
	•	Select your preferred option and click Continue.
Study Period & Session Start
	•	The case scenario will be displayed. You have a 10-minute review time to read the question, draft your answers, and perform any necessary calculations.
	•	You can either wait for the 10 minutes to elapse (the session will start automatically), or you can click Begin Early to jump straight into the active session.
The First Case (Long Form or Short Form)
	•	The active oral board session begins and will last for exactly 35 minutes. This first case will either be a long-form or short-form scenario.
	•	Once the 35 minutes conclude, you will be presented with a choice:
	•	Click Continue Later to pause the exam and immediately view your score for this first case.
	•	Keep Going: Proceed directly to the second case without viewing your score yet.
Completing the Exam & Final Score
	•	If you choose to continue, you will begin the second 35-minute case.
	•	At the end of the full 70-minute exam time (35 minutes for the first case + 35 minutes for the second case), the session will conclude entirely.
	•	You will then be presented with your comprehensive score for both the long-form and short-form cases.
[image:Sub_Step_6.png]

Study Tips for Success
	•	Simulate the Environment: Try to find a quiet space and respond out loud to mimic the actual board experience.
	•	Focus on Diversity: While it is tempting to stick to comfortable topics, ensure you rotate through categories like Pharmacology and Pain Management e.tc. to find and address any knowledge gaps.
	•	Review Feedback: After each session, analyze the evaluation to understand how to better structure your clinical justifications.

The Question Bank is a dedicated practice hub. It allows you to sharpen your knowledge through focused, multiple-choice questions organized by specialized medical and surgical topics.

	•	Trauma

	•	Urology 
	•	ENT (Ear, Nose, and Throat)

Practice Session Workflow
To begin practicing, simply select your desired topic and click Start Practice. The system will guide you through an interactive learning loop:
	•	Answer & Instant Feedback: Select your answer for the question and click submit. The platform immediately validates your choice with clear visual indicators:
	•	If your answer is correct: The option is highlighted in a light green color and marked with a checkmark (✓).
	•	If your answer is incorrect: The option you selected is highlighted in a light red color and marked with an (X) sign
	•	Comprehensive Review Material: Right below the visual feedback, the system instantly displays:
	•	The correct answer
	•	Detailed Explanation: A comprehensive breakdown explaining the underlying medical/surgical concept.
	•	Further Readings: A curated list of reference materials and recommended readings to help widen your knowledge and address any gaps.
	•	Bookmarking: If you encounter a challenging or interesting question that you wish to review later, you can click the Bookmark icon to save it to your personal study list.
	•	Continuous Learning: You can answer as many questions as you like in a single session, moving sequentially from one question to the next.
	•	Ending a Session: There is no fixed time limit or forced ending. You can click End Practice at any point to conclude your session.



MARKET PLACE
The Marketplace is a comprehensive feature, serving as the central hub for discovering examiners, booking sessions, managing schedules, and tracking financial transactions.
The Marketplace is split into three main modules: Find Examiner, Sessions, and Payment.
1) Find Examiner (Public vs. Private Marketplace)
Depending on your account type, your search scope will vary across two marketplace types:
	•	Public Marketplace: Visible to all registered candidates, allowing you to browse and book any examiner publicly available on the Clarilux platform.
	•	Private Marketplace: If you are associated with a specific institution, you will have access to a private marketplace. This space restricts your view exclusively to examiners who are officially affiliated with your institution.
Searching and Filtering 
To streamline your search and find the best match for your study needs, you can narrow down the list of examiners using the following filters:
	•	Topic: Filter by specific medical/surgical topic (e.g., Trauma, Cardiovascular).
	•	Session Tye: 
	•	Availability: Find examiners based on your preferred time slots.
	•	Price: Filter according to your budget or preferred session rates.
	•	Ratings: Source top-rated examiners based on feedback from other candidates.
Reviewing Examiner Profiles & Booking 
Once you select an examiner, you can view their comprehensive profile to make an informed decision. Profiles include:
	•	About Section & Professional Experience: A summary of their background, credentials, and teaching history.
	•	Session Pricing: Explicit costs associated with booking their time.
	•	Cancellation Policy: The rules regarding rescheduling or canceling a booked session.
From here, you can directly select an available time slot, book your session, and proceed to payment.

2) Sessions
Once a session is successfully booked and paid for, it is automatically tracked under the Sessions tab. This area keeps you organized via two main logs:
	•	Upcoming Sessions: A dynamic list displaying all your future, confirmed sessions. You can quickly navigate here to see dates, times, and examiner details.
	•	Session History: A complete archive of all past sessions you have attended or completed on the platform.
[image:Sub_Step_5.png]
3) Payment
The Payment tab serves as your personal financial dashboard within Clarilux. It allows you to monitor and manage all financial data associated with your bookings, offering the following capabilities:
	•	Financial Summary: View your cumulative total expenditure on the platform, as well as an aggregated overview of any refunded amounts.
	•	Status Tracking: Easily monitor the real-time status of your payments across three categories:
	•	Total Payment Made: Completed transactions for confirmed sessions.
	•	Refunds: Fees returned to you due to cancellations or updates adhering to the examiner's policy.
	•	Download Receipts: Access and download official payment receipts for every single session transaction for your personal records or institutional reimbursement.
	•	Financial History: View a quick overview of payment statuses which can be filtered by paid, refunded, failed, disputed and pending and for any transaction listed in your logs, you can directly download an official payment receipt for your personal records

Billing and Subscription
The Billing section, which is accessible via Settings, allows you to manage your subscription, and view payment history.

Selecting a Subscription Plan
If your institution is new to the platform or does not have an active subscription, navigate to the billing section and click View Plans. You can compare different commitment durations to find the best fit for your institution:
	•	Commitment Options: Choose between a Six-Month, One-Year, or Two-Year commitment plan.
	•	Plan Tiers and Seat Capacity: The maximum number of candidate seats available depends on your selected tier:
	•	Basic Plan: Standard features for smaller needs. Up to 30 candidate seats.
	•	Professional Plan: Up to 65 candidate seats.
	•	Premium Plan: Advanced tools for growing programs. Up to 100 candidate seats.
	•	Enterprise Plan: Tailored solutions for large-scale organizations; require clicking Contact Sales to discuss custom requirements.

2. Configuring Your Plan
Once you have selected a plan:
	•	Capacity Adjustment: Adjust the number of candidates associated with your institution. The system supports up to a maximum of 100 seats.
	•	Price Calculation: The total price is automatically calculated based on your selected plan and the number of candidate seats.
	•	Trial Period: Depending on the selected plan, you may be eligible for a Free Trial period.

3. Finalizing Your Subscription
	•	Review and check the box to agree to the Terms and Conditions.
	•	Click Continue to Free Trial to activate your subscription and begin your trial period.

4. Account Overview
Once subscribed, you can return to the Billing page at any time to:
	•	Review your Current Subscription status and plan details.
	•	Access your Billing History for a comprehensive record of all invoices and payments.
